Evidence and limits

  • Six of ten interviewed buyers expressed interest. The sample came from one industry association and includes no signed commitments.
  • A partner estimates a pilot would cost USD 45,000. The estimate excludes local support and contract review.
  • Two customers asked for local invoicing. The team has yet to confirm whether it can meet that requirement.

Dissent to preserve

Finance director. Even a limited pilot could tie up the support team. Require a cost ceiling and signed commitments before expanding.
