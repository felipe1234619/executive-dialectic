01THE THESIS

THE HUMAN CENTER

Technology may accelerate analysis.
Human judgment must remain accountable.

Artificial intelligence can examine alternatives, challenge assumptions, and surface contrary evidence. It cannot quietly inherit the authority or responsibility of the person who decides.

02THE METHOD

A DISCIPLINE OF DECISION

Every consequential judgment deserves a record.

I.

Establish the question.

Define the circumstances, the authority to decide, the available evidence, and the limits of what is known.

II.

Preserve the opposition.

Document alternatives, unresolved disagreement, contrary arguments, and assumptions that could prove false.

III.

Authorize the judgment.

Distinguish analysis from the human act of decision, including its rationale and responsible authority.

IV.

Make revision intelligible.

Retain the history of changes and the conditions under which a judgment should be reconsidered.

03THE INFRASTRUCTURE

JUDGMENT CONTINUITY ENGINE™

The record is not the decision.
It is what makes the decision accountable.
